Fuji as backdrop — hosted an all-Latin-American program from contemporary composers (Ortiz, Márquez, Esteves, Castellanos, Negrón, Sierra) reflecting Dudamel's Venezuelan-rooted repertoire focus, paired with Bernstein's West Side Story Symphonic Dances.
